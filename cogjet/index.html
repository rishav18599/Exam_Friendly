📋 Syllabus
Part I — Aptitude
🔢
Quantitative Aptitude
CAT-level — arithmetic, algebra, data interpretation
📖
Verbal Ability
Reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar
🧩
Logical Reasoning
Puzzles, sequences, analytical problems
Part II-A — Maths & Computation
∑
Discrete Mathematics
Sets, relations, combinatorics, matrices, geometry
∫
Calculus
Limits, differentiation, integration, extrema
📊
Probability & Statistics
Bayes theorem, distributions, expectation, variance, percentiles
💻
Computation & Algorithms
Boolean algebra, data structures, sorting, searching, pseudocode
Part II-B — Cognitive Psychology
👁️
Psychophysics
Absolute thresholds, Weber's Law, Fechner's Law, reaction time
🧠
Learning & Memory
Pavlovian conditioning, Hebbian learning, modal model, working memory
🌈
Visual Perception
Colour, depth & motion perception, perceptual organisation
📉
Research Methods
t-test, chi-square, ANOVA, p-value, effect size, normal distribution
Part II-C — Neuroscience
🫀
Neuroanatomy
Cortical & subcortical structures, brain divisions, PNS, planes of section
⚡
Neurophysiology
Neuron structure, glial cells, action potentials, synaptic transmission
🔄
Brain Function
Regional functions, reflex arc, sensory receptors
